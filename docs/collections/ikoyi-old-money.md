## The story

Different from Lekki. The houses are older, the trees are bigger, the
streets have names that show up in family lawyers' letterheads. If
Lekki is "I made it," Ikoyi is "we've been here." The cars are quieter
too — driven by the same drivers for ten years.

This collection is for people who don't need the postcode to mean
anything new — they want it to mean what it already meant.
