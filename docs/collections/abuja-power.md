## The story

Abuja is not Lagos. The grids are wider, the politics is closer, the
buildings are newer because the city is newer. This collection is for
the people who fly in for Tuesday and out on Friday — diplomats,
contractors with federal business, senior public servants with private
offices, NGO heads.

Where Lagos sells "the dream", Abuja sells "the office". The homes are
backdrops; the work is the point.
